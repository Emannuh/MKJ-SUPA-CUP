Privacy Policy

1. Who We Are

The MKJ SUPA CUP is administered by the County Government of Makueni under the MKJ TINA initiative. This portal (mkjsupacup.com) is operated for the purpose of managing the Governor Mutula Kilonzo Junior Supa Cup competitions across Makueni County.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

  • Team registration data — team name, ward, sub-county, discipline, manager name, email and phone number.
  • Player data — full name, date of birth, national ID number, Huduma Namba, phone number, passport-size photograph, copies of identity documents.
  • Portal user data — email address, role, assigned ward/sub-county, login timestamps and IP addresses.
  • Contact form data — name, email address and message content submitted via the Contact Us form.
  • Technical data — browser type, IP address, pages visited, session duration (standard web server logs).

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and manage team and player registrations for MKJ SUPA CUP competitions.
  • To verify player eligibility (age, identity, higher-league checks) as required by competition rules.
  • To send email notifications related to registration approvals, longlist reviews, fixture schedules, and results.
  • To maintain an audit trail of official actions taken within the portal.
  • To respond to enquiries submitted via the contact form.
  • To improve portal security and prevent fraudulent submissions.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process personal data on the basis of:

  • Legitimate public interest — administering a county government sports programme.
  • Consent — where team managers and players voluntarily submit registration data.
  • Legal obligation — where identity verification is required by competition regulations.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal data. We may share data with:

  • Authorised MKJ SUPA CUP officials (Ward Sports Council Chairs, Sub-County Sports Officers, referees) on a need-to-know basis.
  • The County Government of Makueni for official reporting purposes.
  • Brevo (email delivery provider) — email addresses are transmitted to send transactional emails. Brevo processes this data in accordance with GDPR.
  • Cloudflare — we use Cloudflare Turnstile for spam prevention on the contact form.

6. Data Retention

Player and registration data is retained for the duration of the active competition season and for a minimum of 3 years thereafter for audit and eligibility purposes. Contact form messages are retained for 12 months. Portal activity logs are retained for 12 months.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your data (subject to our retention obligations).
  • Object to processing in certain circumstances.

To exercise these rights, contact us at admin@mkjsupacup.com.

8. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including HTTPS encryption, session management, password hashing (Argon2), and role-based access controls. No system is completely secure; we encourage users to keep their login credentials confidential and to report any suspected security issues immediately.

9. Cookies

This site uses strictly necessary session cookies for authentication. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies. The Cloudflare Turnstile widget on the contact form may set a functional cookie to prevent spam.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the portal after changes constitutes acceptance.

Terms of Use

1. Acceptance

By accessing or using mkjsupacup.com or the MKJ SUPA CUP portal, you agree to these Terms. If you do not agree, do not use the platform.

2. Authorised Use

This platform is provided exclusively for:

  • Ward team managers to register and manage their teams and players for MKJ SUPA CUP competitions.
  • Authorised officials (WSCC, SCSO, referees, administrators) to perform their designated competition management duties.
  • The general public to view fixtures, results, standings and competition information.

3. Prohibited Conduct

You must not:

  • Submit false, fraudulent or misleading registration information.
  • Attempt to access accounts or data you are not authorised to access.
  • Use automated scripts, bots or crawlers to submit forms or extract data.

4. Player Data Accuracy
